Gallery of URLs showing how AI-hip-hop companies construct Black (and female) artist identities. One row per depiction. The field of **Black female** AI hip-hop artists is thin — **Xania Monet** is the principal case (R&B/gospel, not strictly hip-hop). Male-coded artists (FN Meka, TaTa) are included for contrast because they show how the *female figure* and Black identity are constructed in this genre's AI content.

**Reading the gallery:** The only Black *female* AI hip-hop/R&B persona with real commercial traction (Xania Monet) is depicted through (a) a single **AI-generated face** (album art / socials) that is now **contested as appropriating a real Black woman's likeness** (row 4), and (b) **live-action** video of a heartbroken, "wounded-crown" feminine figure (rows 1–2). The male-coded avatars (FN Meka, TaTa) show the genre's broader pattern: Black identity rendered as **cyborg/cyber** (FN Meka) or **sci-fi robot** (TaTa), and the female figure reduced to an **object of display** for the male avatar (row 6).
